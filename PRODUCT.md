# Qirvexa

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated by the user: React, TypeScript and Vite for a reusable small application; Supabase for user data; a Cloudflare Worker for static assets and AI inference.

## Users

Prospective clients and recruiters evaluating a portfolio demo; small service teams are the fictional product audience.

## Product Purpose

Show operational data clearly, manage client projects and turn a short project idea into an editable delivery brief.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Workspace upgrade, 2026-10-24: linkable per-project tasks with status/priority/due date, progress, descriptions/private notes/briefs; a deadline agenda; editable client contact profiles and related projects; recorded contract/paid/outstanding finance views. Guest data stays local; signed-in child records use owner-isolated Supabase tables and composite project-owner references. Unsaved forms survive tab changes and guard page/client navigation. AI briefs can be linked to a selected project after review. No shared-team permissions, invoicing, payment collection, bank integration or real-time collaboration is claimed.
